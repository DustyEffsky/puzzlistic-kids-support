Puzzlistic Kids

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · App version 1.0

Puzzlistic Kids works offline. We do not collect personal information through the game, track players, show ads, or require an account.

Who we are

Puzzlistic Kids is published by Jeremiah Costello under the Fizzwick Wobble name. This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android app, this support site, and support enquiries.

Information in the game

The game stores puzzle progress, onboarding progress, daily picture stamps and preferences on your device so that play can resume. This information is not sent to us. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and does not include advertising, analytics or tracking services. Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases.

Children’s privacy

The game is designed for children aged 4–7. We do not ask children to provide their name, email, age, photos or other personal information. There are no user accounts, chat features or public player profiles. A parent or guardian should handle support enquiries. Please do not send information about a child that is unnecessary to resolve a support request.

Local storage and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until the app’s data is cleared or the app is deleted. On Android, use the system’s Clear storage option to reset progress; uninstalling also removes the app’s local data. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes its local data; offloading an app can retain it. Your operating system may retain or restore data in backups according to its settings. The Android version disables app cloud backup and device transfer. We cannot access or delete device backups for you.

App stores and beta testing

Apple and Google process information when you use their stores, download apps or participate in testing, under their own policies. During TestFlight or Google Play testing, those platforms may make testing information available to us, such as your tester email, installation information, crash reports and feedback. We use information made available through those services to administer testing and improve the app. This is separate from information collected directly by the game.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond, investigate the problem and maintain the app. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store it. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the enquiry, follow up on issues, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting us.

This website

This static site uses no analytics, advertising, embedded media, external fonts or cookies set by us. GitHub Pages hosts it. GitHub receives technical request information, including visitors’ IP addresses, and logs IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Links to other websites open services covered by their own policies.

Your choices and questions

You can remove local game data as described above, choose whether to send feedback, and manage store, diagnostic and backup settings through Apple or Google. For questions about this policy or information you have sent us, contact Jeremiah Costello at mail@jeremiah-costello.com. You may also contact the data-protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. Any future feature that changes how information is handled will be reflected in the policy and relevant store disclosures.
