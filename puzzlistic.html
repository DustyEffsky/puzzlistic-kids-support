Puzzlistic

Support and privacy information for the adult illustrated jigsaw app, package com.puzzlistic.tabletop. Puzzlistic Kids is a separate app.

Support · Privacy policy

Support

Contact mail@jeremiah-costello.com. Please include your device model, operating-system version and what happened. Never send passwords or payment details.

Use How to play in the app’s options menu for dragging, joining, camera controls and accessible grid placement. Your progress is stored locally; uninstalling may remove it.

Privacy policy

Updated 10 November 2026. Publisher: Jeremiah Costello.

Information collected by the app

Puzzlistic works offline. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases. The app does not send puzzle progress, device identifiers or device information to the publisher or third-party services.

Local progress and preferences

Puzzle progress, selected daily puzzle and preferences are stored on your device. Dated puzzles and free play use separate saves. Removing the app may remove this data. Puzzlistic does not provide publisher-operated cloud sync or recovery.

Store services and support

Apple and Google may process store downloads, transactions and diagnostics under their own policies and your device settings. If you contact the publisher, information you choose to send is used to respond to your request.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact mail@jeremiah-costello.com. If the app’s practices change, this policy will be updated.
